   688→1. **Trust Layer approach** - Build zero-retention, auditable AI
   689→2. **Low-code philosophy** - Make AI accessible to non-technical users
   690→3. **In-context assistance** - AI should appear where decisions happen
   691→4. **Confidence indicators** - Always show prediction certainty
   692→5. **Ecosystem thinking** - Design for extensibility from day one

   695→
   696→1. **Domain specificity** - Purpose-built for supply chain, not generic CRM
   697→2. **Real-time first** - Streaming architecture, not batch
   698→3. **Graph-native** - Supply networks are graphs, treat them as such
   699→4. **Deep XAI** - Audit-grade explainability, not just tooltips
   700→5. **Simulation capabilities** - What-if engines, not just predictions
   701→6. **Autonomous agents** - True agentic AI, not just chatbots
